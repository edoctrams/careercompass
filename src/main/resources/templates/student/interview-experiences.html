Google Software Development Engineer (Campus) Batch 2024
Shared by Senior Student • 4 Rounds Evaluated
Difficulty: Hard
The recruitment process was rigorous across 4 distinct rounds: 1. Online Assessment: 2 Medium DSA problems on dynamic programming and graph BFS. 2. Technical Round 1: Interval scheduling and tree traversal with deep focus on time/space tradeoffs. 3. Technical Round 2: Design an in-memory rate limiter with concurrency considerations. 4. Googleyness & Behavioral: Cultural fit, feedback handling, and teamwork. Tip: Clean, modular code and explaining your thinking out loud are heavily evaluated.

Microsoft Software Engineer Intern / FTE Batch 2024
Shared by Student • 4 Rounds Evaluated
Difficulty: Medium
Rounds included: Round 1: Coding test on arrays and string manipulation. Round 2: Data structures (Linked lists, binary search) and OS fundamentals. Round 3: System architecture, databases, and explaining past academic projects. Round 4: Director / HR interview on motivation and leadership. Preparation Advice: Master standard LeetCode mediums and know your resume projects thoroughly.

Amazon SDE-1 Batch 2024
Shared by Senior Student • 3 Technical Rounds
Difficulty: Hard
The interview had 1 OA and 3 technical rounds focusing on DSA and Amazon's 16 Leadership Principles. Every technical round had 20 minutes dedicated to LP questions using the STAR format. Coding problems involved Trie search and Top-K elements using Min-Heap. Key takeaway: Prepare 2-3 genuine project stories aligned with Customer Obsession and Bias for Action.
